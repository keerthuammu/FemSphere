# FemSphere Feature Catalog

## User

### 1. Core Health Twin & Biological Resilience
- **Digital Health Twin Dashboard**:
  - Continuous 3D/digital modeling of hormonal, cardiac, and sleep bio-markers with a dynamic **Biological Resilience Score (0–100%)**.
  - **Gemini Health Twin AI Assistant**: Interactive conversational consultation with continuous clinical bio-synthesis based on current cycle phase and real-time telemetry.
  - **AI Health Twin Insights**: AI-generated clinical insights combining vitals, cycle data, and symptoms with targeted wellness and medical recommendations.
  - **Personal Health Timeline**: Chronological tracking of significant health events, diagnosis dates, milestone achievements, and life events.

---

### 2. Adaptive Life Stage Navigation & Specialized Health Modules
- **Dynamic Biological Life Stage Engine**:
  - Automatically derives the user's biological health stage from Date of Birth (DOB) and clinical milestones (Early Childhood, Pre-Puberty, Puberty, Adolescent Health, Young Adult, Reproductive Age, Perimenopause, Menopause, Older Adult).
  - Eliminates artificial manual dropdowns while supporting physiological onset calibration in Settings & Profile (e.g. adolescent menarche / first period started status).
- **Specialty Health Journeys**:
  - **Pregnancy & Maternity Mode**: Directly integrated with the Period Tracker; activates gestational weeks & days computation, EDD countdown, fetal milestones (fruit size), daily kick counter, and prenatal clinical checklist.
  - **Postpartum Care**: Fourth trimester recovery tracking, pelvic floor rehabilitation, lochia monitoring, breastfeeding/pumping logs, and postpartum depression (EPDS) screening.
  - **Perimenopause & Menopause Navigator**: Vasomotor symptom tracking (hot flashes, night sweats), hormone balance metrics, bone density monitoring, and sleep disruption analysis.
  - **PCOS / Endometriosis Management**: Symptom logging, cycle irregularity assessment, basal body temperature charting, and ovulation flare-up detection.
  - **Puberty & Growing Module**: Adolescent growth tracking, menarche preparation, emotional health, and foundational education.
  - **Early Childhood & Pediatric Care**: Infant and toddler developmental milestones, growth curves, and feeding schedules.
  - **Healthy Aging**: Bone health, cardiovascular wellness, cognitive activity monitoring, and mobility checks.

---

### 3. Menstrual & Reproductive Cycle Tracking
- **Cycle & Ovulation Engine**:
  - Tracking of menstrual phases: Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulation, and Luteal phases.
  - Fertile window predictions, next period forecast, cycle length and period duration calculations.
  - Flow intensity logging (Light, Medium, Heavy, Spotting).
  - Cycle delay adjustment and historical cycle logging.
- **Embedded Pregnancy / Maternity Hub**:
  - Positive pregnancy test logging directly inside the Period Tracker.
  - Automatically pauses menstrual countdown and replaces normal cycle dial with real-time **Maternity & Fetal Hub** (gestational weeks/days, trimester progress, EDD countdown, interactive kick counter, clinical checklist).
  - One-click option to conclude pregnancy and resume standard menstrual tracking seamlessly.
- **Doctor Cycle Sharing**:
  - Secure clinical sharing of cycle history with treating physicians during consultations.

---

### 4. Health Tracker & Daily Vitals Logger
- **Daily Vitals Tracking**:
  - Blood Pressure (Systolic / Diastolic), Heart Rate (BPM), Blood Glucose / Sugar levels, and Body Temperature.
  - Water intake tracking (glasses / mL) with hydration goal progress.
  - Sleep duration (hours/minutes) and sleep quality assessment.
  - Calorie budgeting & energy balance: calories consumed vs. calories burned.
- **Symptom & Mood Journaling**:
  - Multidimensional symptom tracking (cramps, headaches, bloating, breast tenderness, fatigue, acne, back pain, mood swings).
  - Severity scoring, contextual note taking, and trend visualization.

---

### 5. Smartwatch & Universal Wearable Integration
- **Multi-Brand & BLE Wearable Support**:
  - Native BLE pairing and scanning (Bluetooth Low Energy) for fitness bands, smart rings, and smartwatches.
  - Support for Apple Watch, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, and standard BLE GATT Health devices.
  - Pairing via **QR Code Scanner** or Bluetooth discovery.
- **Live Biometric Telemetry**:
  - Real-time 1-second continuous live Heart Rate streaming.
  - Daily step counts with distance (km) and active calories burned.
  - Blood Oxygen Saturation ($SpO_2$) monitoring.
  - Heart Rate Variability ($HRV$) and stress level index.
  - Background synchronization service pushing wearable data to the backend.

---

### 6. Medical Records Vault
- **Encrypted Medical Document Storage**:
  - Document categories: Lab Reports, Prescriptions, Imaging / Scans, Discharge Summaries, and Vaccination Records.
  - Multi-file format support (PDF, JPEG, PNG, DICOM) up to 15MB.
- **AI Document Scanner & OCR**:
  - In-app camera and file scanner with automated OCR analysis extracting key clinical parameters, doctor recommendations, and abnormal lab flags.
- **Dependent Support**:
  - Ability to tag and organize records for dependents.

---

### 7. Clinical Telehealth & Doctor Consultations
- **Doctor Directory & Booking**:
  - Filterable OB/GYN, general practitioner, and endocrinologist directory (filter by specialty, availability, consultation fee, and date).
  - Appointment scheduling with real-time shift and time slot selection.
  - Multi-modal consultation types: Virtual Telehealth, In-Clinic Visits, Home Visits.
  - Appointment lifecycle management: Rescheduling, cancellation, and history review.
- **Integrated Video Telehealth**:
  - In-app virtual consultations with live audio/video controls (Mic Mute/Unmute, Camera On/Off).
  - Incoming call alerts with accept/decline mechanisms and live call status monitoring.
- **Consultation Notes & Digital Prescriptions**:
  - View doctor post-consultation clinical notes, diagnoses, and digital prescriptions directly in the portal.

---

### 8. Prescribed Fitness & Guided Rehabilitation
- **Clinician-Prescribed Workout Plans**:
  - Custom exercise plans tailored to cycle phase, pregnancy trimesters, pelvic floor health, or injury recovery.
- **Interactive Workout Player**:
  - Exercise demonstration cards with step-by-step instructions, target muscles, rep/set counters, rest timers, and audio cues.
  - Exercise logging and completion toggling stored in personal health history.

---

### 9. Partner Mode & Collaborative Family Care
- **Partner Sync & Invite**:
  - Connect with partners or trusted family members via email invitation or pairing code.
- **Granular Privacy & Permission Controls**:
  - Selective data sharing toggles (e.g., share period phase, mood alerts, and fertility status while keeping medical records private).
  - Ability to revoke or modify partner access at any time.
- **Shared Tasks & Goals**:
  - Shared calendar milestones, mutual wellness goals, and cycle care task assignments.
- **Emergency SOS & Care Alerts**:
  - One-tap emergency contact linking and distress notifications.

---

### 10. Clinical AI Reports & Export
- **Comprehensive PDF Health Reports**:
  - Printable and exportable health summaries combining recent vitals, cycle history, wearable statistics, and clinical diagnoses.
  - Secure formatted summaries ready for presentation to healthcare providers.

---

### 11. Notifications Center
- **Smart System Alerts**:
  - Medication reminders, hydration nudges, upcoming appointment notifications, and period onset predictions.
  - Real-time badges for unread alerts with mark-as-read and bulk deletion capabilities.

---

### 12. Privacy, Consent & Profile Management
- **Emergency Medical ID**:
  - Critical clinical profile badge: Blood group, severe allergies, chronic conditions, and emergency contact details for first responders.
- **Privacy Consents & Audit Trail**:
  - Granular consent management for data processing, clinical research sharing, and third-party doctor access.
  - Immutable privacy audit logs tracking data access and permissions.
- **Profile Customization**:
  - Personal health metrics (height, weight, date of birth, blood group), profile avatars, and password security management.
